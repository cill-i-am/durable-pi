- Persist the exact attempt before the native submission starts. Double clicks while saving/sending/acknowledging cannot dispatch twice. Restored uncertainty is inert until explicit Send, then reuses the exact original operation id. No automatic resend or fallback.
- Acknowledgement clears only that attempt and submitted file ids; newer text/model choices survive. Model-unavailable rejection alone clears the attempt after failure. Owner teardown aborts and joins native send/control settlement, fences late acknowledgement, and retains the pending attempt.
