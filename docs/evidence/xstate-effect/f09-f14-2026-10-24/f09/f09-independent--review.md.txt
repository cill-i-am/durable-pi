## Source conclusions

Current authority is read from the actual family actor snapshot, with account-keyed workspace remounting outside this slice. Mutation and model owners check current authority before dispatch and after native completion. Their accepted acknowledgements are separate from ordinary refresh failure.

Invitation generation changes immediately abort the superseded signal; the machine queues the latest source and waits for actual native settlement before advancing. Acceptance is fenced at dispatch, after its response, and after session refresh before URL removal/welcome. Secret tokens/passwords remain ephemeral Redacted values. The only automatic business retry is the one temporary preview retry after 250 ms; the production preview supplies a ten-second abort signal per read. SDK commands and credential exchanges are not automatically replayed. A failed exchange performs one status read and requires the same still-pending attempt before another explicit finish.

An active URL remains usable when storage is unreadable. Popup acquisition stays in the click handler; native pending work and owned blank-popup cleanup remain separate from user-owned navigation.
